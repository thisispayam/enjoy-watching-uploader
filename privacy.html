Privacy Policy: Enjoy Watching Uploader

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Who uses this app

Enjoy Watching Uploader is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Enjoy Watching YT Shorts, to manage that channel. It has no other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos the owner made, to set their title, description, tags and publishing schedule, and to read back the upload's status. It requests only the permission needed to upload to the owner's channel.

What it stores

The OAuth credentials are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never shared, sold or sent anywhere except to Google to make the API requests above. The app does not collect, store or share personal data about anyone, including viewers.

Third parties

Requests go only to Google/YouTube. Your use of YouTube is governed by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account → Third-party connections.
